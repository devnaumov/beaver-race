# Beaver Race

## Register

product

## Users

Players assembling a named crew of up to twelve beavers and watching a short, playful 3D race in a desktop or mobile browser.

## Product Purpose

Make it easy to add participants, shuffle their appearances, start a fair race, follow the standings and celebrate the winner. The seasonal reskin must preserve the race simulation, camera choreography and existing controls.

## Brand Personality

Playful, festive, competitive. The Christmas edition replaces the Halloween theme throughout the interface and scene, with snowy woodland, decorated fir trees, presents and Christmas costumes. Keep the existing English interface copy and recognizable beaver characters.

## Anti-references

No horror, haunted props or Halloween costumes. Avoid replacing the interactive race with a decorative landing page, obscuring racers with seasonal decoration or adding unnecessary steps to starting a race.

## Design Principles

- Keep the race and its participants the focus.
- Carry the seasonal theme consistently through copy, costumes, environment and victory celebration.
- Preserve established controls and race mechanics.
- Keep characters and standings legible on desktop and mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard focus indicators, accessible form labels, error announcements and the existing reduced-motion styles. New ambient snowfall should respect reduced-motion preferences. Do not rely on color alone for race state or standings.
